# Concept round 1: forty premises

Eight independent bots each got the CV (`alex-lewin-cv-data.md`), the same short brief, and one lens. Each returned five text concepts. This is the index: name and premise only.

- ★ marks the concept the bot said it would bet on.
- △ marks the one it said it would be most nervous to pitch.

The brief said: memorable, unique, creative and uniquely his; smart and capable; able to read as professional; tone open. It did not include the business-card story. Off limits were the formal-page-that-flips idea, arcade and 90s-web themes, terminals, fake desktops, bento grids, neon on black, scroll timelines and travel globes.

## 1. Lens: competitor and host (8 wins, 16 hackathons run)

1. **Double Entry.** His record as a double-entry ledger: what he took home on one side, what his events paid out on the other.
2. **The Overlap.** ★ Two overlapping circles, hackathons won and hackathons run, with exactly one event in the intersection: Berlin 2024.
3. **The Organizer's Edition.** "How to Win Every Hackathon" as a short handbook, annotated in the margins by the organizer he also is.
4. **Two Places in the Room.** A hackathon hall drawn as a floor plan with Alex marked twice: at a team table and at the microphone.
5. **The Motion.** △ An Oxford-style debate on "Alex Lewin is a competitor first, a host second", in the format of his own Rough Consensus tool.

## 2. Lens: the "freedom tech" thread

1. **Don't Trust, Verify.** ★ Every claim comes with its receipt, and lines with no public source carry an honest "self-attested" mark.
2. **Seed Phrase.** Alex backed up as twelve words on a steel plate, the way bitcoin holders back up a wallet; each word opens a story.
3. **A Field Manual for Freedom Tech.** A pocket handbook where each chapter is a freedom he has built tools for: to pay, to speak, to bank together, to stay safe.
4. **Rough Consensus.** △ A debate on "Freedom tech needs more builders", with a vote before and after.
5. **Freedom Radio.** A shortwave receiver where each frequency is a channel of his work.

## 3. Lens: a physical object

1. **Specimen Note.** One engraved banknote signed by the Minister of Development, whose security features hold his record.
2. **Both Covers.** ★ A stapled instruction booklet with two front covers, one for winning hackathons and one for running them, meeting at the centre staple.
3. **Tuned In.** A pocket transistor radio; static made of type resolves into readable text as a station locks in.
4. **Checked Copy.** △ The galley proof of a profile of Alex, with the fact-checker's marks left in the margin.
5. **The Editions.** Everything he has run, won and said as a uniform paperback series, with spine width set by participant count.

## 4. Lens: something that unfolds in time

1. **The Hackathon Clock.** ★ One live hackathon that takes the visitor through the stages he runs: kickoff, hacking, expo, finals, awards.
2. **Last Pay Wins.** △ A timer that resets whenever someone pays attention; each click reveals one more fact.
3. **Pleb FM Broadcast.** A live radio station with a schedule; visitors bid to queue the next segment.
4. **The Passport Stamp Tour.** A conducted tour of the eight countries, one short story per city.
5. **Press Release Ceremony.** An awards ceremony where each prize is announced from an envelope, closing with the NPR coverage.

## 5. Lens: a stranger who met him for thirty seconds, on a phone

1. **The Office of the Minister of Development.** His title played straight as a calm public office with three tappable rows.
2. **Run of Show.** A conference-app agenda with a NOW marker that moves through the hackathon day.
3. **The Motion.** △ A vote on "This house should get back to Alex Lewin".
4. **Won / Given.** ★ A two-column ledger: what he has won, and what he has handed to other builders.
5. **Station ID.** Press and hold to hear a 30-second spoken intro, then a queue of segments you can reorder.

## 6. Lens: light background, typography only, printable on one sheet

1. **Both Sides of the Table.** ★ A two-headed page like a playing card: 8 won reads upright, 16 run reads upside down, with Berlin 2024 set sideways across the middle.
2. **The Motion.** "This house would give Alex Lewin thirty minutes."
3. **Notes for the Agent.** △ The text addresses people; margin notes address AI agents, and each note makes the summary more accurate, never more flattering.
4. **The Minister's Portfolio.** A deadpan government paper with numbered paragraphs and annexes.
5. **Index to an Unwritten Book.** The index to "How to Win Every Hackathon", a book that does not exist.

## 7. Lens: one verb

1. **Next Song.** ★ Bid. The site is Pleb FM, and visitors bid on what plays next while they read.
2. **The Motion.** △ Vote. "This house would hire the hackathon winner."
3. **Drop.** Drop a Plinko puck; where it lands decides which part of his record opens.
4. **The Ministry.** File a form at the Ministry of Development; a rubber stamp opens the matching dossier.
5. **Tuning.** Turn a dial; each hackathon city is a station.

## 8. Lens: an exhibition designer who has never built a website

1. **Condition Report.** △ A collection display where every label states what is verified, what rests on his word and what is lost. One plinth is empty.
2. **Push for Light.** A dim room with one brass button shared by every visitor; pressing it lights the cases and resets the timer for everyone.
3. **Where He Stood.** ★ Every hackathon as a floor plan, one dot per participant and one red dot for him.
4. **The Motion.** His workshop title as a motion that visitors vote on entering and again on leaving.
5. **Two Label Heights.** Every object has a second, lower label written for AI agents, and that lower label is the plain record.

## Where independent bots converged

Ideas that several bots reached separately are the ones the CV makes easy to find. They are not necessarily weak, but they are the least likely to surprise.

| Idea | Bots that proposed it |
|---|---|
| An Oxford-style debate with a before-and-after vote (from Rough Consensus) | 6 of 8 |
| A radio or tuning dial (from Pleb FM and the podcast) | 5 of 8 |
| A ministry or government office (from the job title) | 3 of 8 |
| Receipts, fact-checking or provenance marks on every claim | 3 of 8 |
| A two-sided object for competitor and host | 3 of 8 |
| A ledger of won against given | 2 of 8 |
| A hackathon run-of-show or clock | 2 of 8 |
| A shared countdown button (from Last Pay Wins) | 2 of 8 |
| A second layer of notes addressed to AI agents | 2 of 8 |

Ideas only one bot reached: The Overlap, Seed Phrase, Field Manual, Specimen Note, Checked Copy, The Editions, Index to an Unwritten Book, Drop, Where He Stood, Condition Report's empty plinth.
